Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play Catch the Coin?

Use the left and right arrow keys (or A/D) to move the basket. On mobile, tap the screen to move toward that position. Catch falling coins to score — miss 3 and it's game over. Survive all 3 difficulty stages in 60 seconds for the best score.

Is Catch the Coin free to play?

Yes — 100% free. No download, no account, no install required. Open the page in any modern browser and start playing instantly.

Does Catch the Coin work on mobile?

Yes. The game fully supports touch controls on smartphones and tablets. Tap to move the basket, or use the on-screen D-pad. The game enters fullscreen on mobile for the best experience.

How many difficulty levels are there?

3 difficulty stages that progress automatically. Coins fall faster and spawn more frequently each stage — Stage 1 (normal), Stage 2 (faster), Stage 3 (maximum intensity).

How do I get a high score?

Build the combo multiplier by catching coins consecutively without missing — up to ×4. At ×4, a gold coin gives 40 points instead of 10. Catch rare orange bonus star coins (25 pts each) and survive all 3 stages.

Can I install Catch the Coin on my phone?

Yes! Catch the Coin is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On Android, use the browser's "Install App" option. On iPhone Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Is Catch the Coin a coin catcher or coin collection game?

Yes — Catch the Coin is a classic coin catcher and coin collection game. You move a basket to catch falling coins, collect as many as possible, and build your score. It's a lightweight HTML5 arcade game that runs in any browser.

Do I need to download anything to play?

No. Catch the Coin is one of the best no download games — there's nothing to install. It runs instantly in your browser on desktop, phone, or tablet, with no account or login required.

What is the combo multiplier and how does it work?

The combo multiplier rewards you for catching coins consecutively without missing. Your multiplier climbs from ×1 to a maximum of ×4 — at ×4 a gold coin is worth 40 points instead of 10. Miss a coin and your combo resets to ×1.

Is Catch the Coin safe to play?

Yes. Catch the Coin is served over a secure HTTPS connection, runs inside your browser's sandbox, and installs nothing on your device. Your high score is saved locally on your own device — nothing is uploaded.
