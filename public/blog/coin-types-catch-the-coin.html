Coin Types in Catch the Coin — Gold, Silver & Bonus Stars Explained

📅 September 10, 2025 ✍️ Monupatel62 ⏱️ 3 min read

Catch the Coin has three distinct coin types, each with different point values and spawn frequencies. Knowing which coins to prioritise — and when — is key to maximising your score. Here's everything you need to know.

Gold Coins — Your Main Source of Points

Gold coins are the most common coin in the game, spawning approximately 70% of the time. At 10 points each, they may seem modest — but with a ×4 combo multiplier, each gold coin is worth 40 points. Since they spawn so frequently, maintaining your combo on gold coins is the foundation of a high score.

Gold coins have a warm yellow colour (#facc15) and a subtle specular highlight. They're easy to spot at any fall speed.

Silver Coins — Don't Ignore Them

Silver coins are worth 5 points at base value — half a gold coin. Many players assume silver coins are not worth chasing, but this is a mistake. Silver coins still contribute to your combo multiplier exactly like gold coins. Catching a silver coin at ×4 combo gives 20 points and keeps your combo alive. Missing it to avoid "wasting effort" breaks your combo — which costs far more than 5 points.

Silver coins have a pale silver-white colour (#e2e8f0) and are slightly smaller than gold coins. They can be easy to miss at high fall speeds in Stage 3 — stay alert.

Bonus Star Coins — High Risk, High Reward

Bonus star coins are rare (10% spawn rate) and worth 25 points base, or 100 points at ×4 combo. They're marked with a star symbol (★) and have a bright orange colour (#f97316) that makes them stand out clearly.

The key question is whether to chase a bonus star or let it go. The answer depends on your current combo:

  • If the bonus star spawns on the side you're already moving toward — always catch it.
  • If catching it requires a wide lateral movement that risks missing a common coin — skip it, especially in Stage 3.
  • At ×4 combo, a bonus star is worth 100 points — so make a bigger effort to reach it.

Spawn Rate and Combo Strategy

All three coin types count equally toward building the combo multiplier — catching any coin increments your combo counter. This means silver coins are just as valuable for combo building as gold coins. Never deliberately let a silver coin fall just because it's worth fewer points.

In a typical minute at Stage 3 speeds (0.7s spawn interval), approximately 85 coins will fall. With a 70/20/10 split:

  • ~60 gold coins (600 pts at ×1 or 2400 pts at ×4)
  • ~17 silver coins (85 pts at ×1 or 340 pts at ×4)
  • ~8 bonus stars (200 pts at ×1 or 800 pts at ×4)

This shows why maintaining ×4 combo matters so much — catching every coin at ×4 is worth roughly 4× more than playing without a combo.
