Catch the Coin FAQ — Everything You Need to Know

📅 October 2, 2026 ✍️ Monupatel62 ⏱️ 6 min read

New to Catch the Coin, or just have a quick question before you jump in? This FAQ answers everything players ask most about the game — from whether Catch the Coin is free and how to play, to how scoring works, whether it runs offline, and if you can install it as an app. Everything you need to know is right here in one place.

💡 Short version: Catch the Coin is a free HTML5 browser arcade game. No download, no sign-up — just move your basket, catch falling gold coins, and chase a high score in a fast 60-second round.

Getting Started

Is Catch the Coin free to play?

Yes — Catch the Coin is 100% free to play. There is no purchase, subscription, or sign-up required. Open the game in your browser and you can start catching coins instantly.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No download or installation is needed. Catch the Coin is a browser game built with HTML5 Canvas, so it loads directly in your web browser. If you want quicker access you can optionally install it as an app, but that is entirely up to you.

What devices and browsers does it work on?

Catch the Coin works on desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile, and runs in any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, and Opera. On desktop you move the basket with the arrow keys or mouse; on mobile and tablet you use touch controls. If you're deciding which feels better, see our breakdown of keyboard vs touch controls.

Does Catch the Coin work offline?

Yes. Because Catch the Coin is a Progressive Web App (PWA), once it has loaded it can be played without an internet connection. Install it to your home screen or desktop and you can play offline anytime.

How the Game Works

How do you play Catch the Coin?

Move your basket left and right to catch gold coins as they fall from the top of the screen. Each coin adds to your score, and catching coins in an unbroken streak builds your combo multiplier. The goal is simple: score as high as possible before the timer runs out. For a full walkthrough, read how to play Catch the Coin.

How long is one round?

Each round lasts 60 seconds. Within that single minute you move through three difficulty stages that get progressively faster, so focus and pacing matter from the very first second.

How does scoring work?

Every coin you catch is worth points, and that value is boosted by your current combo multiplier. A gold coin is worth 10 points at ×1 and up to 40 points at the maximum ×4. Missing a coin resets your combo, so consistency is the real driver of a high score.

What is the combo multiplier?

The combo multiplier rewards you for catching coins consecutively without missing. The longer your streak, the higher your multiplier climbs — from ×1 up to a maximum of ×4 — multiplying the points each coin is worth. One missed coin resets it back to ×1. We cover this in depth in the combo multiplier explained.

What are the 3 difficulty stages?

A single 60-second round moves through three stages that steadily increase in speed. Stage 1 is the slowest and ideal for building your combo, Stage 2 picks up the pace, and Stage 3 is the fastest and most demanding. Reaching and holding a high combo through Stage 3 is what separates an average score from a great one.

Scores & Progress

Is my high score saved?

Yes. Your personal best is saved locally in your browser, so it persists between sessions on the same device. Clearing your browser data will reset it.

Can I compete with others or is there a leaderboard?

Catch the Coin is a single-player high-score game focused on beating your own personal best. There is no live multiplayer or global leaderboard — the challenge is to keep topping your previous record. Want to climb faster? Try our tips to get a high score.

Technical & Safety

Is Catch the Coin safe to play?

Yes. Catch the Coin runs entirely in your browser, requires no account or personal information, and installs nothing on your device beyond an optional PWA. It is a safe, family-friendly browser arcade game. If you're curious about browser-game safety in general, see are browser games safe?

Can I install it as an app?

Yes. Catch the Coin is a Progressive Web App, so you can add it to your home screen or desktop. On mobile, use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” option; on desktop Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar. Once installed it launches like a native app and works offline.
