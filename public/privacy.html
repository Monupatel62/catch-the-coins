Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2025

Welcome to Catch the Coin ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you visit catchthecoins.netlify.app (the "Site"). By using this Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require an account or any personal information to play the game. The following data may be collected automatically:

  • Server log data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps — collected by our hosting provider (Netlify).
  • High score (localStorage): Your best score is stored locally in your browser via localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server.
  • Advertising cookies: This Site uses advertising networks that may set cookies and collect browsing data to serve ads. See Section 3.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 (via Google Tag Manager) to understand how visitors use the site. This collects anonymous usage data.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve the Site and the game.
  • To display relevant advertisements that keep the game free.
  • To understand visitor behavior via aggregate, anonymous analytics.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

3. Advertising Networks

This Site displays advertisements through third-party ad networks. These networks may use cookies, web beacons, and device identifiers to collect data for ad targeting and measurement.

  • Google Analytics / Tag Manager: Used to measure site usage. See Google's Privacy Policy. Opt out via Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Ad network (push/display advertising): This Site uses an ad network that may request push notification permission and display ads. You may decline push notification permission at any time through your browser settings.

You may opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings or the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out.

4. Cookies

Cookies on this Site come primarily from advertising and analytics services. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect ad functionality but will not affect gameplay.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Netlify — Hosting. See Netlify Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Analytics & Tag Manager) — Analytics. See Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

This Site is a general-audience game not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. GDPR & CCPA Rights

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have rights over your personal data including the right to access, correct, or delete it. Since we collect minimal direct data, most requests can be handled by:

  • Clearing your browser's cookies and localStorage.
  • Opting out of personalized advertising as described above.
  • Contacting us directly (link below).

8. Data Retention

Server log data is retained by Netlify per their standard policy. localStorage data (your high score) is retained on your device until you clear your browser data. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics' standard retention settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance.
