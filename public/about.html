About Catch the Coin

Catch the Coin is a free, instant-play browser arcade game — a classic coin catcher and coin collection game built as a lightweight HTML5 arcade game. Control a basket at the bottom of the screen and catch falling gold coins before they hit the ground. It is one of the best no download games and plays great as a mobile arcade game. Race against a 60-second timer, survive 3 escalating difficulty stages, build a combo multiplier, and beat your personal high score — no download, no account, no install.

How to Play

The goal is simple: catch as many coins as possible in 60 seconds without missing 3. Coins fall from the top of the screen at increasing speeds. Move your basket left and right to catch them. Each miss costs one life — lose all 3 lives and the game ends early.

Desktop — Keyboard ← → arrow keys or A / D keys to move left / right. ↑ ↓ or W / S to move up / down.
Mobile / Tablet — Touch Tap anywhere to move the basket toward that position. Or use the on-screen D-pad (bottom-left).
Pause & Mute Press ESC or P to pause / resume. Press M to mute or unmute sound.
Fullscreen Tap Start Game to enter fullscreen automatically on any device.

Coin Types and Points

Three types of coins fall during gameplay, each with different point values:

Coin Color Points Frequency
Gold Coin Yellow (#facc15) +10 pts Most common (70%)
Silver Coin Silver (#e2e8f0) +5 pts Uncommon (20%)
Bonus Star ★ Orange (#f97316) +25 pts Rare (10%)

Combo Multiplier System

The combo multiplier is the biggest factor in achieving a high score. Catch coins consecutively without missing to increase the multiplier:

  • 3 consecutive catches → ×2 multiplier
  • 6 consecutive catches → ×3 multiplier
  • 9 consecutive catches → ×4 multiplier (maximum)
  • Miss any coin → combo resets to ×1

At ×4 combo, a gold coin gives 40 points instead of 10, and a bonus star gives 100 points instead of 25. Building and maintaining the combo is the key to top scores.

Difficulty Stages

The game automatically progresses through 3 stages during the 60-second session. Each stage lasts 20 seconds:

  • Stage 1 (0–20s) — Normal speed. Coins spawn every 1.2 seconds. Coin fall speed: 180 px/s.
  • Stage 2 (20–40s) — Faster pace. Coins spawn every 0.95 seconds. Coin fall speed: 220 px/s.
  • Stage 3 (40–60s) — Maximum intensity. Coins spawn every 0.7 seconds. Coin fall speed: 260 px/s.

Tips for a High Score

  1. Stay centered — Position the basket in the middle so you can reach coins on either side quickly.
  2. Prioritize combo over bonus coins — A ×4 combo is worth more than chasing a rare star and missing a common coin.
  3. Anticipate Stage 3 — When Stage 3 begins, coins spawn very rapidly. Stay alert and keep movements minimal.
  4. Catch silver coins too — Even at 5 points, silver coins count toward your combo multiplier.
  5. Use fullscreen — Fullscreen gives you a bigger view and more accurate touch area on mobile.

About the Developer

Catch the Coin was designed and built by Monupatel62 as an open-source web game project. Built from scratch using vanilla TypeScript and the HTML5 Canvas API — no game engine, no framework, just pure code.

The project began as a learning exercise in game architecture, real-time rendering, collision detection, and Web Audio API programming. It grew into a polished, fully featured arcade game with a custom 2D engine, particle system, difficulty progression, and PWA support.
