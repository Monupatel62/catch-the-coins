How to Play the Coin Catcher Game

Catch the Coin is a simple coin catch game: coins fall from the top of the screen and you move a basket underneath them. Catch coins before they reach the bottom to increase your score. The game gets faster as you progress.

1. Move your basket

Use the left and right arrow keys or A and D on a computer. On a phone or tablet, tap the screen to move the basket.

2. Catch falling coins

Collect gold, silver and bonus star coins. Each coin type adds a different number of points.

3. Beat your best score

Keep catching coins, avoid misses and build your combo multiplier for a higher score.

Game controls

  • ← / → or A / D: Move the basket left and right.
  • Tap: Move the basket on touchscreens.
  • Esc or P: Pause the game.
  • M: Mute or unmute sound.

Coin Catching, Points and Combo Bonus

Different coins give different points. Catching coins in a row builds your combo multiplier, which can reach ×4. A missed coin resets the combo, so try to keep your catches going.

  • Gold coin: 10 base points.
  • Silver coin: 5 base points.
  • Bonus star: 25 points.
  • Combo multiplier: Builds up to ×4 for consecutive catches.

At a ×4 multiplier, a gold coin can score 40 points. Watch the falling coins and move early when several appear in different places.

Tips for a Higher Coin Catching Score

  • Look ahead and move toward coins before they reach the basket.
  • Prioritise coins that are safely within reach instead of making a risky last-second move.
  • Keep consecutive catches going to build the combo multiplier.
  • On mobile, use short, controlled taps to position the basket.
  • Learn the faster stages so you can react sooner as the game speeds up.
